The Swan Bar — South City / Grafton Architecture & Crawl Connections

Anchored at 57-58 York St, Aungier St, D02 F670 in South City / Grafton since 1661 (365 years of hospitality), The Swan Bar is celebrated for 1661 heritage, 1916 rising bullet scars, victorian marble. One of Dublin's finest preserved Victorian bars. Still has bullet scars from the 1916 Rising in the woodwork. Within the D02 F670 streetscape, its 1916 bullet holes & victorian marble room delivers a verified 9.7/10 Guinness Stout Score™.

Positioned at 53.33962, -6.26555 along 57-58 York St, Aungier St, D02 F670, The Swan Bar connects directly to South City / Grafton's highest-rated stout corridor. Step outside and walk 107m north (1 min) to reach Thomas Moore Inn (★ 9.3), 205m north-east (3 min) to Caribou (★ 9.3), or 210m north-east (3 min) to Peter's Pub (★ 9.6).
