The Stag's Head — South City / Grafton Architecture & Crawl Connections

Established in 1770 and celebrating 256 years in South City / Grafton, The Stag's Head at 1 Dame Ct, D02 P984 embodies victorian elegance tradition. Tucked down Dame Court. Stained glass, Connemara marble counter & mounted stag. Regulars and architecture enthusiasts alike seek out its mahogany bar, live music, snug and cellar-fresh Guinness & Smithwick's Red.

For self-guided route planning from 1 Dame Ct, D02 P984 (GPS 53.34383, -6.26352), three verified heritage taverns sit within immediate reach: head west for 26 metres (~1 min walk) to The Dame Tavern (Stout Score 9.6/10), continue 69 metres north (~1 min) to The Foggy Dew (9.3/10), or stroll 70 metres east (~1 min) to The Bankers Bar (9.4/10).
