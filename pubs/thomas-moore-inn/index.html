Visiting Thomas Moore Inn on a Dublin Pub Crawl

Whether you are stepping into Thomas Moore Inn for an afternoon quiet pint in a snug or making it a core stop along your evening walking route, remember that counter seating operates on a traditional first-come, first-served basis. Order at the bar, allow the bartender the full two-stage pour to let the stout settle, and enjoy the conversation.
