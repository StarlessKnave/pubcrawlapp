Grainger's Pebble Beach — Clontarf / North Bay Architecture & Crawl Connections

Established in 1910 and celebrating 116 years in Clontarf / North Bay, Grainger's Pebble Beach at 18 Conquer Hill Rd, Clontarf, D03 DC97 embodies coastal salt-of-the-earth landmark tradition. Famous Northside seafront tavern revered by pint connoisseurs for pouring one of the creamiest pints of plain in Dublin. Regulars and architecture enthusiasts alike seek out its coastal pint, creamy guinness, locals favorite and cellar-fresh Pint of Guinness & toasted special.

For self-guided route planning from 18 Conquer Hill Rd, Clontarf, D03 DC97 (GPS 53.35949, -6.18968), three verified heritage taverns sit within immediate reach: head west for 1724 metres (~23 min walk) to The Yacht Tavern (Stout Score 9.2/10), continue 1973 metres north-west (~26 min) to Harry Byrnes (9.5/10), or stroll 3131 metres west (~42 min) to Gaffney's Pub (9.4/10).
