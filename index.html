How the Stout Score is Assigned

Pint ratings, cellar quality standards & Dublin heritage benchmarks

The Stout Score is a curated qualitative index evaluating Dublin's best pints of plain. Rather than marketing claims, scores are compiled by cross-referencing traditional cellar infrastructure, certified pour standards, local connoisseurs, and Irish beer media.

The 6 Evaluation Pillars

1. Cellar Care & Short Lines (30%)

Short line distance from chilled cellar kegs to the tap at regulated 6°C preserves genuine nitrogen creaminess.

2. Keg Turnover & Freshness (25%)

High-volume heritage stout houses swap kegs daily or hourly, preventing stale beer sitting in lines.

3. 119.5s Two-Part Pour (20%)

Initial 45° pour, resting settle for dark ruby body development, and a slow top-off without rushing.

4. Head Density & "The Dome" (15%)

A velvety microfoam dome sitting proud of the glass rim that retains creamy lacing all the way to the bottom.

5. Proper Tulip Glassware (5%)

Spotlessly clean, dry, properly chilled official tulip glassware (never warm from a dishwasher).

6. Taste & Nitrogen Balance (5%)

Deep roasted barley warmth, smooth bitterness, and silky carbonation with no metallic taste.

Score Tiers in Dublin

9.8 – 9.9 The "Holy Grail" Pint (The Long Hall, Gravediggers, Mulligan's, Bowe's)
9.5 – 9.7 Heritage Connoisseur Standard (Kehoe's, Toner's, The Swan, The Palace)
9.3 – 9.4 Solid Traditional Dublin Local (Brazen Head, Cobblestone, Lord Edward)
8.8 – 9.2 High-Volume / Tourist & Craft Pubs (Temple Bar Pub, Merchant's Arch)

Dublin Pub Crawl Generator · Best Dublin Pubs & Guinness Crawl Map

Dublin Pub Crawl is an interactive web utility and curated guide mapping 118 historic Dublin heritage pubs. Explore rooftop-accurate satellite walking routes, real-time pub opening hours, Guinness stout quality scores, and instant 1-tap Google Maps walking directions.

Curated Dublin Pub Crawl Routes & Themes

  • The Classic Heritage Crawl: Dublin's definitive historic pub tour featuring Bowes Lounge, The Palace Bar, The Long Hall, Kehoe's, and Mulligan's of Poolbeg Street.
  • Traditional Irish Music Trail: Authentic live acoustic trad sessions at The Cobblestone in Smithfield, O'Donoghue's on Merrion Row, and The Piper's Corner on Marlborough Street.
  • Victorian Snug Trail: Hidden private wooden snugs preserved from the 19th century at Kehoe's, The Stag's Head, Ryan's of Parkgate Street, and Slattery's of Beggar's Bush.
  • Literary Dublin Pub Crawl: Historic drinking dens frequented by James Joyce, Brendan Behan, Flann O'Brien, and Patrick Kavanagh including Davy Byrnes, The Palace Bar, and Neary's.
  • Historic Liberties Pub Trail: Traditional working-class Dublin pubs outside the tourist zones, including John Fallon's 'The Capstan Bar', The Lord Edward, and The Spire.

Top Dublin Pub Districts & Quarters

  • South City Centre & Grafton Street: Home to The Long Hall, Kehoe's, Neary's, Peter's Pub, and The Stag's Head.
  • The Liberties: Historic brewing and distilling heartland featuring Fallon's, The Lord Edward, and proximity to the Guinness Storehouse.
  • Smithfield & Stoneybatter: Bohemian Northside village featuring The Cobblestone, Frank Ryan's, The Glimmer Man, and L. Mulligan Grocer.
  • Georgian Quarter: Elegant heritage pubs including Toners on Baggot Street, O'Donoghue's, and Doheny & Nesbitt.
  • Glasnevin: Home to John Kavanagh 'The Gravediggers', celebrated for pouring one of Dublin's finest pints of Guinness stout since 1833.

Guinness Stout Scores & Pint Pouring Quality

Every curated pub on Dublin Pub Crawl is evaluated using the 5-point Stout Score methodology: Glassware cleanliness and head retention, cellar temperature (6°C), line freshness and high keg turnover, the traditional two-part pour (119.5 seconds), and the velvety creamy dome.
