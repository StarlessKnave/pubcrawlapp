Frequently Asked Questions

Why is John Kavanagh (The Gravediggers) rated so highly for Guinness? ▾

The Gravediggers maintains natural cellar cooling directly through subterranean stone foundations, immaculate copper lines, and an uncompromising multi-generational pouring standard.

What makes a pint score 9.8+ or 9.9 on the Stout Score? ▾

Near-zero head shrinkage, deep ruby color against backlighting, velvety micro-foam dome extending slightly above the rim, and dense rings of lacing with every sip.

How do I get between The Gravediggers and the city center stops? ▾

A quick 10-minute bus (Route 4, 9, or 83) or taxi connects Glasnevin to South City, or enjoy a scenic walk through the Royal Canal.

Do these pubs serve food alongside stout? ▾

Mulligan's and Bowes are strictly traditional wet houses focusing solely on the pint. The Gravediggers serves acclaimed Dublin coddle and tapas in its lounge.
