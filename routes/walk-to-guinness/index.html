The Walk to Guinness (Storehouse Route)

Scenic Liberties Heritage Walk directly to St. James's Gate

The perfect pilgrim's walk to the Home of Guinness. Starting near Christchurch Cathedral, this trail winds down historic Thomas Street through traditional pubs before delivering you right at the gates of St. James's Gate.

Stops 5 Heritage Pubs
Total Walk 1.6 km
Walk Time ~20 mins
Est. Duration ~3.0 hrs

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Does this crawl end at the Guinness Storehouse? ▼

Yes! The final stops on Thomas Street are less than 300 meters from the Guinness Storehouse entrance at St. James's Gate.

2. Should I book my Guinness Storehouse tickets in advance? ▼

Yes, Storehouse tickets frequently sell out days in advance, especially afternoon time slots with Gravity Bar access. Book online prior to starting your crawl.

3. Can I bring bags or luggage on this pub crawl? ▼

Most heritage pubs have limited cloakroom space. If you have large bags, use the luggage lockers at Heuston Station or the Guinness Storehouse coat check.

4. How long should I spend at each pub on the walk? ▼

Allocate 35 to 45 minutes per stop. That gives you plenty of time to savor the two-part pour without feeling rushed before your brewery tour.
