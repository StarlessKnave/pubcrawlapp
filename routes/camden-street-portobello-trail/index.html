Camden Street & Portobello Trail

Live Music Hubs, Vinyl Lounges & Southside Buzz

The beating heart of Dublin's modern Southside entertainment strip. Camden Street packs indie gig temples, Victorian heritage gems, and late-night culinary hot spots along one walkable avenue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Whelan's internationally famous? ▾

Whelan's is Ireland's iconic live indie music venue, launching the careers of Damien Rice, Glen Hansard, and featured in films like P.S. I Love You.

What is special about The Bleeding Horse? ▾

Dating back to the 17th century, The Bleeding Horse is one of Dublin's oldest coach stops, mentioned in Joyce's Ulysses and Sheridan Le Fanu's Gothic novels.

How late are Camden Street pubs open? ▾

Most venues on Camden Street hold late licenses, serving until 2:30 AM or 3:00 AM on weekends.

Is food readily available late at night? ▾

Camden Street is Dublin's premier late-night food strip, packed with shawarma, pizza, and burrito joints open until 4:00 AM.
