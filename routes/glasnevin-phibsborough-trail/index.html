Glasnevin & Phibsborough Pilgrimage

The Gravediggers, Royal Canal & Europe's Longest Bar

Escape the tourist hub for a pilgrimage to the soul of Dublin pub culture. Anchored by John Kavanagh (The Gravediggers) beside Glasnevin Cemetery and the historic Hole in the Wall bordering Phoenix Park.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is John Kavanagh nicknamed 'The Gravediggers'? ▾

It shares a boundary wall with historic Glasnevin Cemetery. In the 1800s, cemetery gravediggers would knock on the pub's rear wall to have pints passed through to them.

What is the Hole in the Wall? ▾

Bordering Phoenix Park, Hole in the Wall dates to 1651 and is known as Europe's longest pub, boasting cozy fireplaces, vintage antique clutter, and excellent dining.

Are there TVs or music at The Gravediggers? ▾

No. The public bar has no TV, no piped music, and no wifi—preserving 1833 atmosphere where conversation and the pint take center stage.

Is this crawl walkable in a single evening? ▾

The stops cover 7.2 km total. Many visitors walk the canal stretches or take a quick 5-minute taxi between Glasnevin and Phoenix Park.
