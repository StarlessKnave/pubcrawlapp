Live Trad Music & Fiddle Trail

Acoustic Sessions & Ballad Haunts

Immerse yourself in Dublin's living acoustic trad culture. From spontaneous reels around the front table in Smithfield to the legendary birthplace of The Dubliners on Merrion Row, this route delivers unamplified music and creamy stout.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do these music pubs charge an admission fee or cover charge? ▾

No! Traditional Irish music sessions in Dublin pubs are completely free. It is customary to buy drinks at the bar and contribute a few euros to the musicians' tip jar.

What instruments will I hear on this trail? ▾

You will regularly hear fiddle, uilleann pipes, tin whistle, bodhrán, tenor banjo, accordion, and acoustic guitar played in informal session circles.

Where is the best session on this route? ▾

The Cobblestone in Smithfield is globally renowned as the cathedral of Dublin trad, hosting sessions 7 nights a week from late afternoon until closing.

Can I join in the session if I play an instrument? ▾

Yes! Trad sessions welcome guest musicians. It is polite to listen for a tune or two first, introduce yourself to the session leader, and join in when invited.
