Traditional Irish Music Trail

Live Acoustic Trad Sessions & Fiddle Haunts

Immerse yourself in Dublin's living acoustic trad culture. From spontaneous reels around the front table in Smithfield to the legendary birthplace of The Dubliners on Merrion Row, this route delivers unamplified music and creamy stout.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do these music pubs charge an admission fee or cover charge? ▼

No! Traditional Irish music sessions in Dublin pubs are completely free. It is customary to buy drinks at the bar and contribute a few euros to the musicians' tip jar.

2. What instruments will I hear on this trail? ▼

You will regularly hear fiddle, uilleann pipes, tin whistle, bodhrán, tenor banjo, accordion, and acoustic guitar played in informal, collaborative session circles.

3. Where is the best session on this route? ▼

The Cobblestone in Smithfield is globally renowned as the cathedral of Dublin trad, hosting sessions 7 nights a week from late afternoon until closing.

4. Can I request songs during a traditional session? ▼

Trad sessions are collaborative jam sessions among musicians rather than concert performances. It's best to listen and applaud between tunes; unsolicited pop song requests are politely discouraged.
