Smithfield & Stoneybatter Trail

Indie Craft Hubs, Trad Sessions & Cozy Snugs

Named by Time Out as one of the coolest neighborhoods in the world, Stoneybatter pairs authentic Victorian drinking dens with modern craft beer gardens and wood-fired sourdough pizzas.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get to Smithfield from Dublin city center? ▾

Take the Luas Red Line tram from Abbey Street directly to the Smithfield stop (under 7 minutes), or take a pleasant 15-minute walk along the River Liffey.

Does L. Mulligan Grocer serve craft beer or standard Guinness? ▾

L. Mulligan Grocer is famous for supporting independent Irish microbreweries and craft cider makers alongside extensive whiskey and culinary pub dishes.

Are there outdoor beer gardens on this trail? ▾

Yes! Bonobo boasts one of the largest heated beer gardens in North Dublin.

What is unique about The Glimmer Man? ▾

The Glimmer Man is an eccentric treasure trove of retro Irish memorabilia, vintage signs, and Vietnam-inspired street food served from the back courtyard.
