Artisan Poitín, Speakeasies & Velvet Salons

Experience Dublin's internationally acclaimed mixology renaissance. From Bar 1661 (crowned Ireland's Bar of the Year for native poitín craftsmanship) to 1930s art deco luxury at The Sidecar and the whimsical multi-story snugs of Peruke & Periwig.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Irish Poitín and why is Bar 1661 famous for it? ▾

Poitín is Ireland's original distilled spirit, banned in 1661 and legalized in 1997. Bar 1661 is the global flagship for artisanal poitín cocktails, highlighting its earthy, complex barley flavor.

Is there a dress code for these cocktail lounges? ▾

Smart casual is ideal. Neat jeans and clean trainers are welcomed in Bar 1661 and Peruke & Periwig, while 9 Below and The Sidecar have a slightly more upscale evening atmosphere.

What should I budget per cocktail on this crawl? ▾

Craft cocktails in Dublin's premier lounges average €14 to €17 each, crafted with house-made syrups, bespoke bitters, and premium spirits.

Are non-alcoholic craft cocktails available? ▾

Yes! All venues feature dedicated zero-proof menus crafted with distilled botanical spirits and house infusions.
