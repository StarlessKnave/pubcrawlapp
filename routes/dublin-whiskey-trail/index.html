The Dublin Whiskey Trail

Rare Single Malts & Historic Cellar Vaults

Discover Ireland's liquid gold through centuries-old whiskey houses. From the 150+ bottle selection in Bowes Lounge to the subterranean 18th-century wine cellars of The Merrion, this trail is dedicated to premium single pot stills and whiskey lore.

Frequently Asked Questions

What style of whiskey should I order in Dublin? ▾

Ask for Single Pot Still Irish Whiskey (e.g. Redbreast, Green Spot, Powers John's Lane). It is Ireland's unique historic distillation style made with malted and unmalted barley in copper pot stills.

Do I need prior whiskey knowledge to enjoy this trail? ▾

Not at all. Bartenders at Bowes, Moss Lane, and the Irish Whiskey Museum are exceptionally knowledgeable and eager to recommend pours based on your flavor preferences.

What is the average price of a premium whiskey dram? ▾

Standard Irish whiskeys run €6 to €8, while aged single pot stills and rare cask finishes typically range from €9 to €16 per measure.

Can I get beer or Guinness at these stops as well? ▾

Yes, every stop on this trail pours exceptional draught Guinness and craft beers alongside their whiskey cabinets.
