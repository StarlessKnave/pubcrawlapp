Frequently Asked Questions

Are drinks more expensive in Temple Bar? ▾

Yes, pubs directly on Temple Bar's central square charge €8.50+ for a pint. Our route balances these with authentic fringe pubs like The Palace Bar that charge standard Dublin rates.

What makes The Palace Bar special on this route? ▾

Established in 1823 on the edge of Temple Bar, The Palace Bar is a magnificent, untouched Victorian time capsule celebrated for world-class Guinness and whiskey.

What is the Porterhouse known for? ▾

The Porterhouse opened in 1996 as Ireland's original craft brewpub, brewing its own famous Oyster Stout and Plain Porter on multi-level timber floors.

Is live music available along this trail? ▾

Yes! The Temple Bar Pub, Merchant's Arch, and Darkey Kelly's offer continuous live acoustic Irish music throughout the afternoon and evening.
