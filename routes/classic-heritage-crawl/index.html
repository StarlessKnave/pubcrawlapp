Frequently Asked Questions · The Classic Heritage Crawl

Where does The Classic Heritage Crawl begin and finish in Dublin? ▾

The Classic Heritage Crawl starts at The Brazen Head (20 Lower Bridge St, Merchants Quay, D08 WC64) in The Liberties / Christchurch and finishes at The Palace Bar (21 Fleet St, Temple Bar, D02 H950) in Temple Bar, covering 6 curated stops across 2582m (~33 minutes of walking).

Which pub on The Classic Heritage Crawl has the highest Guinness Stout Score™? ▾

The Long Hall (Est. 1766) holds the highest cellar rating on The Classic Heritage Crawl at 9.9/10, while the full 6-stop route averages 9.60/10.

What is the oldest historic pub along The Classic Heritage Crawl? ▾

The oldest licensed house on The Classic Heritage Crawl is The Brazen Head at 20 Lower Bridge St, Merchants Quay, D08 WC64, dating back to 1198 (828 years of Dublin tavern history).

Do you need bookings for the 6 stops on The Classic Heritage Crawl? ▾

All 6 pubs on The Classic Heritage Crawl (The Brazen Head, The Long Hall, The Stag's Head, Kehoe's, Grogan's Castle Lounge, The Palace Bar) welcome walk-in pint drinkers without reservations; starting between 4:00 PM and 5:30 PM gives you the best shot at snug and counter seating.
