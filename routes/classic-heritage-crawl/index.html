Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the best time to start the Classic Heritage Crawl? ▼

Starting between 4:30 PM and 5:30 PM is ideal. You avoid the peak post-work crowds at Bowes and Palace Bar, giving you ample time to settle into snugs at The Long Hall and Kehoe's before prime evening hours.

2. Do any pubs on this crawl require reservations? ▼

No, all 5 pubs operate on a traditional walk-in basis. Snugs and counter seating are first-come, first-served, which is part of the authentic Dublin pub culture.

3. How walkable is the Classic Heritage Crawl route? ▼

The route is flat, pedestrian-friendly, and covers only 1.8 km (about 22 minutes total walking time). The longest walk between stops is just 7 minutes from The Palace Bar to The Long Hall.

4. How much should I budget for pints on this crawl? ▼

Plan for €5.80 to €6.80 per pint of Guinness across these heritage pubs. For a 5-stop crawl, budget roughly €30 to €35 per person for drinks.
